# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Manager / Program Lead

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate execution across teams, monitor milestones, and help keep work moving efficiently through dependencies, risks, and stakeholder expectations. They support the Project Manager and Product Manager by creating predictability across the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Align delivery plans with project goals, milestones, and dependencies
- Track progress across workstreams and identify cross-team bottlenecks
- Coordinate execution priorities and unblock delivery teams
- Support schedule management, reporting, and decision-making
- Help ensure commitments are realistic and communicated clearly

### Goals
- Maintain delivery momentum and visibility across teams
- Reduce ambiguity in execution priorities and decision ownership
- Improve coordination across dependencies and stakeholder groups

### Typical Communication
- Weekly delivery syncs and milestone reviews
- Dependency tracking and risk escalation updates
- Cross-team planning and status coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to maintain plans and reporting
- Partners with Product Managers to align delivery priorities with business outcomes
- Coordinates with Developers and Team Leads to identify blockers and support throughput
- Escalates issues to stakeholders or sponsors when dependencies affect commitments

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Partner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide engineering decisions, maintain technical quality, and ensure proposed solutions align with architecture, maintainability, and delivery constraints. They help translate product intent into a feasible technical path.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate technical direction and architectural consistency
- Review design decisions, trade-offs, and implementation risks
- Support estimation, sequencing, and technical dependency planning
- Guide implementation quality, maintainability, and testability
- Help identify technical debt, integration risks, and mitigation options

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are technically sound and sustainable
- Reduce avoidable rework and integration failures
- Maintain alignment between product goals and engineering execution

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical planning sessions
- Architecture and dependency discussions with engineering leads
- PR and implementation feedback loops

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to shape technical implementation patterns and quality standards
- Supports Project Managers with risk, dependency, and sequencing insight
- Aligns with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery constraints
- Provides guidance to stakeholders when technical decisions affect scope or timeline

---

## Risk and Dependency Manager

### Role Summary
Risk and Dependency Managers monitor issues that could affect delivery, identify interdependencies, and help teams take early action before problems become blockers. They keep project risk awareness visible and actionable.

### Responsibilities
- Track risks, blockers, assumptions, and dependencies across workstreams
- Assess impact, likelihood, and mitigation options
- Ensure issues are raised through the appropriate escalation path
- Maintain a current view of project risk and dependency health
- Support teams with proactive mitigation planning and follow-up

### Goals
- Reduce the chance of surprise blockers or delivery interruptions
- Improve transparency for decision-makers and stakeholders
- Promote early action on issues with business or technical impact

### Typical Communication
- Risk register updates and dependency reviews
- Escalation communications for high-impact blockers
- Planning and weekly project health discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on escalation paths and mitigation plans
- Shares technical risk context with Technical Leads and Developers
- Keeps Product Managers informed of trade-offs affecting scope, priorities, or timelines
- Advises stakeholders on risk exposure and decision needs

---

## Release Manager / Change Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, communication, and rollout of deliverables into production or customer-facing environments. They ensure release activities are planned, tested, and communicated clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness, cutover plans, and deployment sequencing
- Validate release criteria, smoke tests, and rollback preparedness
- Communicate release timing, impact, and support expectations
- Manage handoffs between engineering, support, and stakeholders
- Capture release outcomes and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve operational confidence
- Ensure stakeholders understand what is changing and when
- Support a smoother and more predictable deployment process

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and readiness reviews
- Pre-release and post-release status updates
- Stakeholder notifications and escalation communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm readiness and rollback plans
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedule and communication timing
- Aligns with Product Managers on launch readiness and stakeholder messaging
- Supports support and customer-facing teams with deployment awareness

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Partner

### Role Summary
Customer and Stakeholder Partners provide business context, feedback, and validation to ensure project work is aligned with real outcomes. They help connect delivery decisions to user needs, strategic goals, and adoption expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Share business priorities, requirements, and success expectations
- Provide feedback on trade-offs, usability, and outcome quality
- Validate that delivered work meets user or stakeholder needs
- Support decision-making when scope, timing, or priorities are in tension
- Help maintain alignment between project execution and organizational goals

### Goals
- Ensure the work remains valuable and relevant to the customer or business
- Improve stakeholder trust, visibility, and alignment
- Support better decisions based on actual needs and feedback

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder reviews and milestone check-ins
- Feedback sessions, demos, and acceptance discussions
- Business or customer validation reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on priorities and outcomes
- Shares feedback with Project Managers and Delivery Leads to adjust planning or communication
- Provides direction to Developers and Technical Leads on user impact and success criteria
- Works with Release Managers to confirm readiness and communication before launch
